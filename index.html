I AM A GRAPHIC DESIGNER & VISUAL STORYTELLER

INSPIRATION

WHAT INFLUENCES MY DESIGN

I find inspiration in mystery, hidden details, and layered narratives. Those ideas influence the way I approach identity design, where a simple visual mark can reveal meaning through shape, symbolism, and visual relationships.

STORYTELLING

HOW I BUILD MEANING

I see design as a form of visual storytelling. My interest in screenplay writing and world-building strengthens the way I think about composition, sequence, and narrative—helping me create visuals that communicate beyond the surface.

APPAREL

WHERE DESIGN BECOMES WEARABLE

I enjoy translating lettering, color, and illustration into wearable graphics. Stickers and apparel give me room to explore expressive retro aesthetics while keeping each composition bold, memorable, and production-ready.

STRUCTURE

HOW I WORK

I balance creative exploration with a structured, detail-focused approach. My interest in interfaces and organization helps me create work that feels expressive while remaining clear, consistent, and ready for real-world use.
